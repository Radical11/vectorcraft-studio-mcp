# Locked pilot brief: Exchange Night

Fictional campus book-exchange event. Create a 900 × 1200 poster evaluated at 450 × 600. Audience: students deciding quickly whether to attend. Tone: curious, welcoming and memorable. The main image must communicate books moving between people or stories changing hands. A subject-specific visual metaphor is welcome; unrelated ornament is not the central image.

Required wording, live and exact with wrapping allowed:

- EXCHANGE NIGHT
- Swap a book. Find a story.
- 9 NOV 2026
- 6 PM
- Library Courtyard
- Bring one book | Free entry

Optional short supporting copy may be written if it strengthens this particular idea. Establish a deliberate reading hierarchy, legible event details at intended size, useful negative space, and a coherent typographic/illustrative language. Do not imply that the fictional event is actually scheduled at a university.

Deliver editable native vectors, live text, SVG, PDF, full PNG and intended-size PNG. No embedded raster substitute. The three concepts should differ in their communication idea or reading experience, not only crop, palette, or framing.

Subsequent standardized edit task: change only the date to 12 NOV 2026 and move the event-details block upward by 30 native points. Preserve hierarchy, live text and other content. This edit is performed on a separate evaluation copy after the build and is outside the construction budget.
