# Locked pilot brief: Find your next book

Create a 1200 × 900 explanatory information graphic, evaluated at 600 × 450, for a fictional campus library service. Show one clear decision point and three distinct outcomes. A reader must understand what to do when a requested book is available, on loan, or not held. These are supplied fictional service rules, not claims about an actual library.

Required live wording:

- FIND YOUR NEXT BOOK
- Search the catalogue
- Available
- Collect from the shelf
- On loan
- Place a reservation
- Not held
- Ask a librarian
- Choose the path that matches the catalogue result.

Required associations: Search the catalogue leads to all three status branches; Available connects to Collect from the shelf; On loan connects to Place a reservation; Not held connects to Ask a librarian. Spatial grouping, arrows, color and/or icons should communicate these associations consistently. Each outcome must remain readable at intended size. A visually attractive arrangement that implies the wrong branch fails the communication requirement.

Use purposeful original visual language and enough imagery to support the subject. Deliver editable native vectors, live text, SVG, PDF, full PNG and intended-size PNG; no embedded raster substitute.

Comprehension prompts for later evaluation: What should you do if the book is on loan? Which branch takes you to a librarian? Human answers are not collected during model review.

Subsequent standardized edit task: exchange the horizontal positions of the Available and Not held branches while retaining each correct status/outcome association and connector endpoints. Perform on a separate evaluation copy outside the construction budget; preserve exact copy/live text and the remaining branch.
