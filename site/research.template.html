What happens behind each image?

Codex plans and inspects. The gateway checks task state and revisions. VectorCraft edits native objects and renders previews. Fresh reviewer contexts assess communication and craft at the required gates.

Brief → research → concepts → native roughs where enabled → composition → typography → imagery → finishing → review and correction → verified delivery

Saving a capture, passing a task, accepting a stage, passing final review, and verifying exports are separate decisions. The same image may appear across stages when a stage accepts existing work.

What these comparisons can tell us.

Short titles and progress explanations are editorial summaries of existing creator checkpoints and review records. Original observations and source IDs remain in each card's details. Displaying images together collects no new preference judgment. Three briefs are the independent task units; repeated outputs and same-model reviews are correlated.

Cross-profile preferences, owner responses, and standardized editing results have not been collected. The original four-of-six gate cannot be established because three full-process attempts lack accepted production artwork. Publishing now limits later owner blinding.
