# Peer review questionnaire

Voluntary manuscript review. Do not supply personal identifiers. No responses have yet been collected. This questionnaire evaluates the paper and proposal; it is not a completed human artwork study.

For each item select **clear**, **partly clear**, **unclear**, or **not assessed**, then cite a page, figure, or claim when suggesting a correction.

1. Does the paper clearly separate the released companion from the proposed algorithm and published neural systems?
2. Can you trace significant software and historical claims to the evidence index?
3. Are the 124 Windows checks, 113 self-contained checks, and visual observations distinguished convincingly?
4. Are the matched comparisons and remaining repair weaknesses reported fairly? What confound is missing?
5. Do the eight technical figures explain responsibilities, state changes, semantic entities, and the two graph types?
6. Would the proposed layout and blockout stages expose a failed hand–tool–object relationship before detail work? Explain a likely failure case.
7. Is the decomposition rule practical for coherent editable contours? Suggest an example of over-fragmentation.
8. Is the unrun 54-output protocol feasible and fair under matched budgets? Identify the most useful measurement or strongest bias risk.
9. Are literature claims supported and abstract-only access disclosed adequately?
10. What is the highest-impact revision needed before academic submission?

Optional background: no design experience / some coursework or practice / sustained design practice / prefer not to say.

Overall recommendation: suitable after minor revisions / substantial revision needed / insufficient evidence / not assessed.

Please distinguish a factual correction, a methodological concern, and a personal visual preference. Negative findings are not required when a section is already clear. Do not interpret this form as consent to publish your response; obtain separate permission before sharing any reviewer comments.
