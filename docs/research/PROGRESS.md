# Progress and stage comparisons

**15 October 2026:** ten of eighteen attempts are accounted for; eight remain deferred. Four passed final construction review, one delivered files with unresolved acceptance, one retained partial artwork, and four were disrupted without final artwork.

The work has progressed from an existing designer workflow to isolated native roughs, focused task/stage acceptance, and explicit relationships between meaningful parts. These capabilities are implemented and technically tested. Their comparative design benefit remains unestablished.

## How to read the evidence

Follow an attempt from its available roughs through composition, typography, imagery, finishing, and corrections. Captures preserve the original order and confirmation status. A later version is not automatically better; the retained best can be an earlier version. Some stages have multiple captures and some have none.

Cross-profile views group the same brief **and repeat**. Missing slots remain visible. Different repeats are separate attempts and are not substituted to create complete pairs. These are descriptive side-by-side displays, not newly collected preference results.

Stage captions identify the existing record they summarize. They do not add new aesthetic scores or independent reviews. A confirmed image means the recorded capture was confirmed; it does not imply final acceptance. Native reopening and export checks establish file properties rather than communication quality.

The [interactive research page](../../site/research.html) supports stage and trial selection with full/intended-size previews. Serve `site/` locally to use the controls. The public showcase follows deployed main, while this research work stays on the draft branch.

The additive [stage supplement](../../public-evidence/process-stages-20261015/README.md) indexes 56 existing captures: 17 roughs, four representative construction studies, and 35 production/correction captures. It does not replace the original checkpoint. Its [publication audit](../../public-evidence/process-stages-20261015/publication-audit.json) discloses the earlier JSON normalization/redaction hash differences.

## What is unfinished

- Eight builds, all formal cross-profile judgments, owner responses, and standardized editing evaluation remain pending.
- Three missing full-process final outcomes already prevent establishing the original four-of-six gate. The threshold stays unchanged.
- Publication creates prior exposure; later owner feedback cannot be described as guaranteed blind.
- Saved-selection recovery, selection-sensitive area-text preflight, and an honest voluntary unresolved-quality stop remain post-study proposals.
