# Visual release protocol

The three fixed holdout briefs in benchmarks/briefs31 were locked before designer-v1 tuning. They are separate from calibration references. Baselines use the preserved pre-3.1 gateway; improved candidates use designer-v1. Required content, canvas dimensions and viewing sizes are equal. These are creator-authored case studies, not randomized independent-agent trials or a general proof of professional superiority.

Both routines receive critique and consequential corrections. Preserve original outputs and the strongest reviewed version. Reviewers actually inspect neutral full/use-size previews and document inspection, with brief/factual constraints but without creator scores, histories or intended verdicts. Two fresh specialists review construction; separate fresh reviewers evaluate randomized final comparisons.

Launch requires both comparison reviewers to find clear improvement on at least two of three briefs and no consequential regression on the remaining brief. Different style, more detail, changed scores or target fidelity alone do not count. The owner then reviews one compact anonymous set. The owner selected the retained designer-v1 version in all three anonymous comparisons on 2026-10-12; this completes the planned artwork gate.

Record actual visual observations separately from fixture tests, render-file hashes and production checks. Native files must reopen with live text and editable meaningful components; vector finals and SVG/PDF exports contain no embedded raster substitute. Inspect exported SVG/PDF/PNG visibly at intended size. Report research shortfalls, translation losses, unresolved weaknesses and fairness limitations. Never silently loosen the gate when it fails.

Local candidate evidence is an explicitly sanitized derivative in public-evidence/. The Windows suite passed 124 checks; these include self-contained fixtures and actual native integration. Fresh-session routing selected Studio for a substantial request. A separate gpt-6-luna quick edit changed only three ellipse color channels and the document modification timestamp, without research or geometry/text changes.

Two fresh blind reviewers observed all twelve matched full/use images independently. They agreed on clear common-brief improvements for all three locked briefs and found no consequential regression in the selected improved versions. This satisfies the independent comparison gate; the owner subsequently preferred the retained designer-v1 version in all three anonymous pairs. Target fidelity is a separate result: the repair construction exhausted three correction rounds and retains moderate fidelity, simplified forearm anatomy and spare-tool geometry. It is delivered with those weaknesses rather than labeled fully resolved.

The three historical briefs were also revisited through neutral comparisons. The original infographic remains stronger. Poster judgments were equal or preferred the original. Both reviewers found improved subject recognition in the repaired editorial, together with a consequential style-cohesion regression. Historical outputs/records remain untouched, and those pairs are not advertised as universal improvement.

Research coverage was three inspected calibration references per brief, below the broader 15–25-work target. Shared-vault lessons were retrieved during critique rather than freshly retrieved before initial construction. These limits and the creator-authored evaluation design restrict the conclusions. All six selected native files were reopened; text-bearing examples retain live Type objects, SVG retains text, and native/SVG/PDF files contain no embedded raster content. Export observations are agent-attested; PDF previews use independent Poppler rendering and SVG previews use the native importer.
