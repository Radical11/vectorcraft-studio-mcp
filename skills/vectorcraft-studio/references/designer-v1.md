# Brief-specific design judgment

New full runs use designer-v1. `workflow_status` provides authoritative schemas. Historical runs retain their contract; do not retrofit evidence or bypass the new profile for substantial new work.

## Calibrate decisions

After research, select three to five actually inspected references relevant to the brief. Record `calibration`: source URL, local observed image, visible relationship, transferable principle and its application. References can teach different things; they are not a template or universal style. Disclose gaps. Counts, hashes and adjectives do not establish quality.

| Genre | Priorities | Resolve | Common weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| expressive-poster | Event recognition, image/copy relationship, atmosphere at viewing distance | Focal silhouette, negative space, subject-specific metaphor, logistics hierarchy | Stock slogan, decorative icon, title crowding image |
| editorial-illustration | Narrative, gesture, form and depth | Hands/tool contact, proportions, overlap, structural contours, light/shadow masses | Fragmented tracing, disconnected anatomy, generic scenery |
| information-graphic | Beginner comprehension, causal relationships, scan order | Labels and qualifications beside mechanisms/directional marks | Decoration replacing explanation, vague title, unlabeled arrows |

## Develop alternatives

Make three developed layouts with actual copy, different composition and image treatment. Symbolic sketches and recoloring do not constitute resolved alternatives. Evaluate editable copy for specificity, clarity, tone, illustration relationship and subject substitution. Preserve required wording. Wordless work explains why copy is inapplicable.

Give a fresh independent concept reviewer only the brief, alternatives and calibration images. They actually inspect all images and assess brief fit, composition, image treatment and copy. Record `concept_review` with current hashes, observations and selected direction. The host selects autonomously after considering critique; the reviewer is not the user. If all are weak, explore once more. After two passes disclose weaknesses rather than endlessly making nominal variants.

## Prove construction

Build a separate representative study before substantial imagery, using a scoped quick planning document rather than the final or original. Record `construction_study`: native file, full/use images, actual component IDs, spec hash and observed silhouette, spacing, proportions, gesture, contour and detail checks. Reconfirm after spec revisions. Limitations may be empty when none is observed.

Prefer native paths for meaningful focal forms. Flat art may justify tracing after a pilot; texture-heavy renders need selective reconstruction. Generate targets separately from live text. Preserve recognizable subject mechanics rather than thousands of irrelevant colored fragments. Inspect target and render after meaningful stages and repair accidental losses immediately.

## Compare common-brief suitability

Final records contain fresh communication/art-direction and drawing/production reviewers. Both inspect every neutral image, target and native measurements. They return eight criteria, copy, fidelity and `brief_comparison`; previously unreviewed baselines need full assessments. Unknown original target permits null fidelity. Target mismatch cannot establish common-brief failure.

Packets contain A candidate, B retained best and C original when distinct. Common-brief winner is candidate, incumbent, original or equal. Disagreement retains the incumbent. `best_version` reflects the overall winner, including originals. A stylistic tie is not improvement. Three correction rounds and the two-non-improving stop remain. Verify actual reviewer tool use; fixture observations are not image evidence.
